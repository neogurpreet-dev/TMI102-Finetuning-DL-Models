Nothing is hardcoded

The weights from our three trained models are loaded in your browser and run on whatever image you choose, including ones we have never seen.

Honest about the images

The folder holds 30 random galaxies from the held-out test set (10 per type), not picked to look good. Some predictions will be wrong, mergers most often.

What the heat map means

Grad-CAM shows where the network's last layer found evidence for a class. Bright patches pushed the score up. It is coarse (7×7) and shows what the model used, not proof it reasoned correctly. Click any class bar to see the evidence for that class.

Compressed for the web

Weights are stored as 8-bit numbers to keep the download near 50 MB. On our 30 test images this matched the original models on 89 of 90 predictions.
